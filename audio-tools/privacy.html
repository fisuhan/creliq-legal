Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Creliq ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Audio Tools mobile application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

Information We Collect

Audio Tools does not require an account.

  • If you contact support, we may receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send.
  • The app may collect anonymous diagnostics such as device model, operating system version, app version, crash reports, and usage analytics.

Audio Files

Audio files you choose remain on your device. Files are processed locally unless a future feature explicitly states otherwise. We do not upload your audio files to our servers.

Permissions

  • Access audio files you select
  • Save exported files
  • Display notifications for background processing

Analytics

We may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to improve the application.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are securely processed through Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. We never receive or store your payment card information.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Play Services
  • Firebase
  • RevenueCat

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
