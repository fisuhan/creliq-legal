Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

This policy explains how data associated with the Video Tools application can be deleted. Video Tools is operated by Creliq.

No User Account Required

Video Tools does not require an account, so there is normally no Video Tools user account stored by Creliq that needs to be deleted.

Videos, Audio and Media Files

Video Tools includes Video Compressor, Video Cut & Trim, Video Converter, Video Merger, and Video to Audio. Media processing is performed locally on your device. Creliq does not upload or store copies of the media you process on our servers.

Deleting Processed and Exported Files

Generated files are stored on your device and can be deleted using Video Tools where available, your file manager, gallery/media application, or Android storage tools. Uninstalling Video Tools may not delete files exported to shared device storage.

Deleting App Data

  1. Open Android Settings.
  2. Go to Apps.
  3. Select Video Tools.
  4. Open Storage.
  5. Select Clear Storage or Clear Data.

You can also uninstall Video Tools. Exported files may remain and can be deleted manually.

Analytics and Crash Data

Video Tools may use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics and diagnostics may be retained by Firebase according to Google's policies. Because the app does not require an account and analytics may be anonymous or pseudonymous, individual records may not always be identifiable as belonging to a specific person.

Purchase Information

Purchases and subscriptions may be processed through Google Play and RevenueCat. These providers may retain purchase records for billing, accounting, fraud prevention, legal, or other legitimate purposes. Uninstalling the app does not delete Google Play purchase history.

Support Information

If you contact us by email, we may retain your email address and correspondence for support, security, or record-keeping. You may request deletion of personal information associated with your support correspondence.

How to Request Data Deletion

Email support@creliq.com with the subject Video Tools - Data Deletion Request. Include enough information to identify the relevant support correspondence or data. Do not send passwords, payment card information, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Data We May Need to Retain

Some information may need to be retained where required for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution, or enforcement of applicable agreements.

Contact Us

For questions or deletion requests, contact Creliq at support@creliq.com.
