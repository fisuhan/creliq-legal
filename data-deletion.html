Data Deletion Policy

Effective Date: August 7, 2026

Audio Tools primarily processes audio files on your device.

Local Data

Your audio files remain on your device. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete exported files.

App Data

You can remove locally stored preferences and cached data by clearing the app storage or uninstalling the application.

Analytics

Anonymous analytics and crash reports may be retained according to Firebase retention policies.

Purchases

Subscription records are managed by Google Play and RevenueCat. Cancellation should be completed through your Google Play account.

Request Data Deletion

If you previously contacted support and would like personal information associated with your support request deleted, email support@creliq.com.
